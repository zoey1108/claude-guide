Privacy Policy

Applies to the Prompt Academy (提问学堂) iOS app and website · Effective October 13, 2026 · 中文版

We don't collect any personal information

Prompt Academy requires no sign-up or login. It does not collect, upload, sell or share your name, contact details, device identifiers, location or any other personal information.

Your learning records stay on your device

Your progress, quiz answers, saved items and anything you type into templates are stored only in the app or browser storage on your device. They are never sent to any server. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data deletes them too. You can also delete them at any time with “Clear learning records” on the Me tab.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Clipboard

The app writes text to the system clipboard only when you tap “Copy.” It never reads your clipboard.

About the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you visit it, GitHub and Google may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. The iOS app bundles all of its content and makes no such requests.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

For any privacy questions, please open an issue on the GitHub project page.
